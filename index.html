Aevalspansion

A small real-time strategy game about territory. Push your border out, claim the gold, and break the red HQ.

ExpandEvery building and unit projects territory. You can only build and mine inside your own colour.
MineWorkers mine gold from mines inside your territory. The two centre mines are neutral until someone claims them.
DestroyChain outposts toward the enemy, train soldiers there, and destroy the red HQ before yours falls.

How to play

Destroy the red HQ before red destroys yours. You can see the whole map, including every enemy unit and building. Hover anything for details.

Territory

Every HQ, outpost, commander and troop projects influence around itself. Whoever has more influence owns that patch of ground, shaded in their colour with a bright border line. You can only build inside your territory and workers can only mine from mines inside it. Marching troops forward moves the border with them. The commander projects a huge radius, so it is your best territory tool.

Buildings

HQ trains workers and one commander. Outposts (100 gold) train soldiers, accept gold from workers, and must be placed within range of your HQ or another outpost so they form a chain. Soldiers trained at an outpost belong to its garrison: select the outpost and right-click the map to send the whole garrison at once.

Units

Worker 50g. Mines gold, builds outposts. Mines on its own when idle.
Soldier 60g. Fights anything that comes near, then returns to its post.
Commander 150g. Strong, big territory radius. One at a time.
Gold mine. Number shows gold left. Outline shows who owns it.
Courier (Courier mode only). Carries orders from HQ along the outpost chain. Can be killed.

Courier mode

An optional harder ruleset. You still see everything, but you can only directly command units inside the HQ's dashed sight circle. Orders for an outpost's garrison go into an outbox at the HQ. The courier picks them up a few seconds later (or press D to send it now), visits only the outposts that have orders by the shortest route, delivers each one, and returns. Give orders any time: if the courier is away, they wait for its next trip.

The courier is fast on your territory, slow on red territory, and has only 25 HP. If it dies, the orders on board are lost and a replacement appears after a few seconds. Click the courier to see what it carries or to call it back. Red plays by the same rule, so hunting the red courier (the white diamond) stops red's waves before they start.
